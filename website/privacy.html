Privacy Policy

Effective date and last update: 20 September 2026

This policy covers niyetsen.com and the Niyetsen iOS/Android apps (package com.niyetsenai / com.niyetsen.app). No login is required to read it. Controller: Şahin Çelebi. Requests: ai@niyetsen.com. Written for Google Play Data Safety, the App Store, KVKK, GDPR/UK GDPR and CCPA.

1. Data we process

  • Account: name, email, Apple/Google identifiers, Supabase user id.
  • Profile: date of birth, zodiac, timezone, language, optional gender for address only (“kadın” / “erkek” / “belirtmek istemiyorum”).
  • Content: chat, plans, tasks, mystic guide.
  • Photos: in-app camera only for task proof or fortune. No gallery access. No biometrics or face identification.
  • Gameplay: points, streak, category progress, excuses, optional nickname league (alias only).
  • Subscription: plan status and store transaction ids. No card numbers on Niyetsen servers.
  • Technical logs: session, security, crash. Website early-access form only if you submit it.

We do not collect precise location, do not use an advertising id, do not sell data, and do not share it for cross-context ads.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Account, tasks, points: contract (KVKK 5/2-c; GDPR 6/1-b).
  • AI chat, plans, fortune, proof photos: consent (KVKK 5/1 / 6; GDPR 6/1-a / 9).
  • Security and debugging: legitimate interests (GDPR 6/1-f).
  • Store billing records: legal obligation (GDPR 6/1-c).

4. Recipients

Google Gemini (replies; we do not train our own model on your data), Supabase, Railway, Apple / Google Play / RevenueCat (they control payments), Unsplash (plan images only), FormSubmit if you send a web form, PostHog/Sentry if configured, authorities if required. Transfers may leave the EEA under GDPR Arts. 44–49.

5. Retention

  • Account: membership + up to 3 years after a deletion request.
  • Chat, plans, fortune: + up to 1 year after deletion.
  • Proof photos: up to 1 year or until account deletion.
  • Points / league alias: + up to 2 years.
  • Invoices: as tax law requires (often up to 10 years, usually at the store).
  • Security logs: up to 6 months.

6. Your rights

KVKK Art. 11; GDPR / UK GDPR Arts. 15–21; CCPA know/delete/correct. Email ai@niyetsen.com (subject “Privacy Request”). Reply within 30 days. Complaints: KVKK Board, CNIL, BfDI, ICO as applicable. We do not sell California personal information.

7. Fortune, children, permissions

Tarot, coffee, palm and horoscope are entertainment — not medical, legal or financial advice. The service is 18+ only. Under-18 data is deleted if discovered. Camera is used only when you take a proof or fortune photo. Notifications are optional. Location is not requested.

8. Account deletion

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. Or the public page account-deletion.html (no login). Deletion is free. Web requests complete within 30 days after we can verify the email.
