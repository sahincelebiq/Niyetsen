Account and data deletion

Effective date and last update: 6 September 2026

Google Play and App Store account-deletion page. Privacy: Privacy Policy.

1. In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Niyetsen and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

2. By email

Write to ai@niyetsen.com with subject Account deletion and your registered email. We complete verified requests within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Account, profile, chat, plans, proof photos, points, streak, consents, fortune logs, league alias.

4. What may remain

  • Store invoices as tax law requires (often up to 10 years; cancel subscriptions in Apple/Google settings).
  • Security logs up to 6 months.
